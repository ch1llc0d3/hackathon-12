Ventanilla

An agent that stands at the counter with you.

Drag this to your bookmarks bar:

Ventanilla

Then open any official form and click it.

  1. Keep the Docker-hosted Ventanilla agent running (docker compose up -d).
  2. Go to the form you are stuck on.
  3. Click the Ventanilla bookmark. A panel opens on the right.
  4. Say which language you want, and press Read this page.
  5. For a screen the form reader cannot see, opt in to screenshot sharing and press Entender una captura. Choose a tab or screen in the browser prompt.

Screenshot privacy: a shared image is sent to OpenRouter's vision model and may contain anything visible on the chosen screen. Close or cover private details first. Normal form reading sends only labels and structure.
